# User manual

## Browse

The home page highlights PMICBook with a direct link to its published overview chapter, displays all five published covers with their individual chapter counts, and links to subject discovery, the lifelong Learning Journey, and Little Explorers. The candidate catalog snapshot contains five published books, 74 chapters, and 20 verified experiment anchors; confirm these totals against the integrated catalog before publishing. Select Explore the library to search registered books. Mathematics proposals, CuriosityBook, and the Kids & Family drafts are separate from the book catalog and do not count as publications.

## Search and filter

Search title, subtitle, description, chapter titles, topics, and tags. Narrow results by subject or publication status. Clear filters resets all controls. Current filters are reflected in the query string and can be bookmarked.

## Follow subject and learning links

Subjects (`categories.html`) show which knowledge areas have a published textbook connection. Select Semiconductor Engineering for its knowledge map or Mathematics to open its concept map. Published map items link only to current published books. The Learning Journey (`learning-journey.html`) lets you choose a guidance stage and one of eleven subjects, explore prerequisite/revisit connections, and see whether real chapter material exists, a book is only planned, or a concept has no authored book yet. Stage navigation is flexible guidance, not a readiness judgment. Learning paths (`paths.html`) route sequences of reviewed chapters in published books. Little Explorers (`kids-family.html`) starts with interests and story themes, then links a few existing preschool concept IDs to their explicit later-stage revisits. Only the three HeavyVehicleBook stories have complete drafts; other interests show an honest empty state. Caregiver and factual notes are available before reading, while prompts and picture details can be skipped. Use **Print or save this bilingual story** to print all spreads or save a local PDF for offline read-aloud.

## Experiments

Experiments (`experiments.html`) lists twenty anchors verified against the published book sites: four from PMICBook, six from WATBook, three from ProcessIntegrationBook, four from FabControlBook, and three from ThinFilmBook. Select Open experiment to jump to the corresponding interactive chapter section.

## Language and appearance

The language button switches between English and Korean and remembers the choice in this browser. The theme button switches between light and dark and remembers that preference. If browser storage is unavailable, both controls still work for the current page. The separate [reader specimen handoff](../design/reader/handoff.md) documents a local bilingual light/dark reader prototype and integration constraints for a future textbook owner; it is not a deployed route or a change to any published textbook.

## Feedback and book status

Feedback opens a public GitHub issue form. Issue titles, descriptions, attachments, and your GitHub username are public; do not include private or confidential information. Roadmap entries are proposals, not available textbooks. The Learning Journey stores no child profile or progress and requests no identifying information. A published title links to its repository and production site.
