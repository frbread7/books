## University mathematics sequence example

[MIT Bulletin 2026–2027, Mathematics Course 18 degree chart](https://catalog.mit.edu/degree-charts/mathematics-course-18/) and [Course 18 subject catalog](https://catalog.mit.edu/subjects/18/), reviewed 2026-10-12, provide one current example of course relationships among calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability/statistics, and numerical analysis. This is not a universal prerequisite standard. The roadmap treats prerequisites as recommended preparation, notes where topics can be studied in parallel, and allows institutions to choose different sequences.

## Review and maintenance

`reviewDate` and each reference’s `reviewedOn` field are machine-validated. Review these sources annually before school-year planning and whenever MOE, NCIC, the National Education Commission, NAEYC, National Academies, or university catalogs publish a relevant update. Record the new exact notice/edition and access date; retain superseded versions in the history rather than silently changing their meaning. Never use the portal’s concept map as an official standards-compliance claim.
